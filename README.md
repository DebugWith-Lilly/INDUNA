# ✦ INDUNA

### *Community Resource & Support Platform*

---

## ✦ Problem Statement

Many individuals and families struggle to access essential resources and services due to financial barriers, limited awareness, and a lack of accessible information.

People seeking assistance often struggle to find affordable services, while donors, sponsors, and willing volunteer drivers lack a centralised way to connect with communities that need support.

There is a need for an integrated platform that connects people in need with available resources, financial contributions, sponsorships, and transport assistance.

---

## ⌖ Our Solution — INDUNA

### *Connecting Communities. Empowering Lives.*

INDUNA is a community-driven platform designed to connect individuals with essential resources, services, and community support.

The platform brings together four interconnected user groups through several key capabilities:

- **Resource Discovery** — Find free and affordable healthcare, food, education, and community services.
- **Assistance Requests** — Allow individuals to request support when accessing essential resources.
- **Donor Portal** — Enable individuals to contribute financially towards community needs.
- **Driver Network** — Connect people requiring transport assistance with volunteer drivers.
- **Sponsor Portal** — Allow organisations and businesses to support community initiatives.
- **Impact Tracking** — Monitor contributions, assistance requests, and community impact.
